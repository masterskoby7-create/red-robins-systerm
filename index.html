About

Legal Notice:

Red Robin's Access System is designed for monitoring and managing YOUR OWN devices and network connections only. You must own the device or have explicit written permission from the owner to access, view, or interact with any device, network, file, or signal through this system. Accessing or monitoring devices, networks, or files that you do not own or have permission for is illegal, unethical, and may result in legal action. Unauthorised use will result in permanent loss of access. By creating an account, you confirm that you will use this system responsibly and only with devices and data that belong to you or that you have clear permission to use.

Red Robin's Access System gives you full visibility and control over your own laptop computer, your internet connection, nearby Bluetooth devices, and any external devices you plug into your computer. It shows you what your own device is doing, what signals your computer can detect around you, what connections your computer is making to the internet, and lets you safely view, manage, and transfer files on devices that belong to you. This system does NOT allow you to access, control, or monitor other people's devices. That is illegal and this tool is not designed for that purpose.
